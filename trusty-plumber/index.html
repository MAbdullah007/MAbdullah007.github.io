Water where it shouldn't be?

  1. Turn off the water at the nearest shutoff valve — or the main, if you're not sure which one.
  2. Move anything valuable out of the water's path and put a bucket under active drips.
  3. Call us at (817) 587-2852 and describe what you see. We'll tell you the next step.
