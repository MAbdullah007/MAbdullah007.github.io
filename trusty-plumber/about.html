About Trusty Plumber

Trusty Plumber is a plumbing business based at 405 South Main Street in Fort Worth. We do repairs, new installations, and routine maintenance for homes in Fort Worth and the surrounding area, and we're open 24 hours, seven days a week.

The work we take on

Most of our calls are the unglamorous kind, and that's fine — a leaking pipe, a toilet that won't stop running, a new fixture to install, a water heater due for attention. It's the plumbing a house depends on every day, and it's work where doing it carefully matters more than doing it fast and hoping.

What you can expect

When you call, you'll reach the business directly — not a call centre. We'll ask what's happening, give you our honest read on how urgent it is, and explain the fix and the cost before work begins. If something can safely wait, we'll tell you. If it can't, we'll tell you that too, and why.

We'd rather explain a problem clearly than sell you a bigger job than the one you have. Houses are expensive enough without paying for repairs you don't need.
