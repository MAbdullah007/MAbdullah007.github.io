Services at MR AUTO LLC

Four things, done under one roof in Dallas: repairs, body work, insurance claim repairs, and used car sales. Here's what each one actually involves, so you know what you're calling about.

Car Repairs & Maintenance

This is the everyday work: the car won't start, the brakes are grinding, a warning light is on, or it's simply due for maintenance. Our technicians run engine diagnostics to find the fault instead of guessing, then explain what needs fixing now and what can wait.

The shop handles tune-ups, oil changes, brakes, tires, suspension, transmissions, engine work, mufflers, A/C service, and diagnostics. Typical repair work includes engine diagnostics, brake repairs, and routine maintenance to keep the car safe and reliable. If a repair isn't worth doing — it happens with older cars — we'll tell you that too, and you can look at what we have for sale instead.

Body Work & Collision Repair

After a collision, the visible damage is only part of the story. We handle dent repairs, paint work, and frame straightening, so the car looks right and tracks straight on the road again.

Bring the car in for a look before you decide anything. We'll document the damage, walk you through what the repair involves, and give you a straight answer on the work needed. Most collision jobs here are paid through insurance — see the next section.

Insurance Claims

Filing a claim and getting a car repaired is two jobs, and most people only signed up for one of them. We work directly with your insurance company to complete the repair after an accident, and we help you save on your deductible where the claim allows.

You bring us the car and the claim details; we handle the back-and-forth with the insurer about the repair itself. If you're not sure whether to claim or pay out of pocket, call (972) 764-5820 and we'll talk through both honestly.

Cars for Sale

We also sell used cars — sedans, SUVs, and other everyday vehicles that have been inspected and maintained before they're offered for sale. Because the same shop services what it sells, we know the cars on the lot better than a seller who just bought them at auction.

Inventory changes as cars come and go, so the fastest way to find out what's available is to call. Tell us your budget and what you need the car for, and we'll tell you plainly if we have something that fits.

Not sure which one you need?

That's normal — most people aren't. Call (972) 764-5820, describe what's going on with the car, and we'll point you to the right service. Or send the details through our contact page.
