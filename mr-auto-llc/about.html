About MR AUTO LLC

MR AUTO LLC is a family owned and operated auto repair and body shop on Belleview Street in Dallas — the family has run it since 1985.

The shop takes all major credit and debit cards.

MR AUTO LLC is an auto repair and body shop on Belleview Street in Dallas. We do the work that usually sends people to three different places: mechanical repairs, collision and body work, and the insurance claim that pays for it — plus a small lot of inspected used cars for sale.

Why one shop for all of it

After an accident, a car often needs more than body work. Something underneath is bent, a light is on, the alignment is off. When the body shop and the mechanic are the same people, nothing gets lost between two businesses, and you make one phone call instead of four.

The same thinking applies to the cars we sell. They're inspected and maintained by the technicians who work here, so we can answer questions about a car's condition with a straight face.

What to expect when you call

You'll talk to someone at the shop who can actually answer questions about your car. We'll ask what happened or what the car is doing, tell you what we'd need to look at, and explain the next step before you commit to anything. If a repair doesn't make sense for the car's value, we say so — and if we can't help, we say that too.
