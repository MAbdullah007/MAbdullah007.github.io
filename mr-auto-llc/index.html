Had an accident? Here's how it usually goes

  1. 1. Call us. Tell us what happened and what shape the car is in. If it isn't safe to drive, say so — we'll talk through your options.
  2. 2. Bring the car in. We look it over, document the damage, and explain what needs to be done before any work starts.
  3. 3. We deal with the claim. If you're going through insurance, we work with your company on the repair and keep you posted.
  4. 4. Pick up a repaired car. Body work, paint, and mechanical repairs handled under one roof, so you're not shuttling between shops.
