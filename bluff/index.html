How to Play Bluff

Goal: be the first to get rid of all your cards — but your last play has to survive.

The basics

  • One 52-card deck is dealt out completely to 2–6 players.
  • On your turn, play 1–4 cards face down, then declare any rank you like — select two Kings and declare "two Queens" if you dare. Your real cards stay secret; only the count and your declared rank are public.
  • Sequence style (chosen when the game is created): the first play of a round declares any rank, and each later play in that round must declare the next rank up.

Opening rule

  • The first play on an empty pile must be at least 2 cards (2, 3 or 4). Only exception: if you hold just one card in total, you may play it.
  • Once the pile has cards, you may play a single card again.

Check (challenge) or Pass

  • After every play, each other player in turn order chooses CHECK (challenge it) or PASS.
  • The first CHECK flips the played cards and compares them with the declaration: if they really are the declared rank, the play was honest and the checker takes the whole pile. If even one card is wrong, it was a bluff and the player who played takes the whole pile.
  • Whoever wins a challenge leads the next round.

Everyone passed? Burn the pile — or add more

  • If everyone passes your play, the turn comes back to you with a choice:
  • BURN PILE — the whole pile leaves the game for good (burned cards never come back), and you open a fresh play. The opening rule applies again: at least 2 cards.
  • ADD MORE CARDS — keep the pile and play more cards on top of it, with a new declaration. The others respond again — and if they all pass again, you choose again.

Four of a kind — the instant burn

  • Playing 4 cards declares four of a kind of your chosen rank.
  • If someone CHECKs, the normal challenge above decides who takes the pile.
  • If everyone PASSes, the claim is believed and the whole pile is burned automatically — no choice needed. You open the next round.

Winning

  • Playing your last card doesn't win by itself. You win only if that play survives: everyone passes, or a CHECK shows it was honest.
  • If a CHECK catches your last play as a bluff, you take the pile and the game goes on.

Example turn

The pile is empty, so the opening needs 2+ cards. Maria plays 2 cards — really two Kings — but declares "two Queens". Tom PASSes. You hold three Queens yourself, so her claim is impossible — you CHECK. The cards flip: K, K — a bluff! Maria takes the whole pile, and you lead the next round. If everyone had passed instead, Maria would choose: burn the pile and open fresh, or add more cards on top.
